# Claims and review status

## What this note establishes conditionally

Theorems 1 and 3 (numbering may change with revisions) concern a pressure integral satisfying an explicit radial envelope and the resulting leading local analytic fixed-point problem. The source's equations and coefficient-space structure are attributed to its Appendix A/B. Our contribution is an explicit sufficient certificate, not a new existence method.

The external source is not independently certified. Global outer admissibility, source separation, endpoint continuation/shear, higher-order coefficients, and completed cutoff tails remain outside this certificate. No programmable device or standard-model computational speed-up is established.
